# Life OS Community Library

A shared index of flashcard decks and practice exams for
[Life OS](https://github.com/jcranokc/obsidian-life-os).

**This repo hosts no content.** It is a list of pointers to decks that live in
their authors' own repositories. You keep your work, your licence and your
history; you can update or withdraw it at any time by editing your own repo or
removing one line here.

## Using a deck

In Obsidian: **Life OS → Library**, browse, and install. Decks land in your
configured study folder as ordinary Markdown.

Or just clone the author's repo and copy the files in yourself. There is nothing
proprietary about the format.

## Sharing a deck

The fastest route is inside the plugin: **Life OS → Library → Share a deck**.
It walks you through it and writes the files for you.

By hand, it is four steps:

1. **Put your deck in a public GitHub repo.** One Markdown file is enough.
2. **Add a LICENSE.** `CC-BY-4.0` is a good default for study material.
3. **Fork this repo** and add one entry to `library.json` — see
   [SCHEMA.md](SCHEMA.md).
4. **Open a pull request.**

## What belongs here

Decks you wrote, or that you have the right to share under the licence you
declare. Written from public exam guides, official documentation, textbooks you
are paraphrasing, or your own experience.

**Not** questions copied from paid practice exams or commercial question banks.
That is infringement, it puts the uploader at risk, and it makes everything else
here impossible to trust. Entries found to be copied are removed.

## Quality

Nobody vets correctness. `author` and `license` are how you decide whether to
trust a deck, and the repo link is right there — read a few cards before
committing to 300 of them.

If a deck has errors, open an issue on the **author's** repo, not this one.
